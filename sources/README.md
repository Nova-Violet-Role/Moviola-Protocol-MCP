## ⚖️ How disagreements resolve

> **The bench page outranks the vision page, always.**
> Where MASTER_VISION speaks of +90 fps 1-bit streams and
> MOVIOLA-PROTOCOL.md measures 10 fps and 8 colours as the floor,
> the shipped presets follow the measurement — and the 90 fps
> 1-bit form ships too, as the Canine Tessersect, at the size the
> bench says it costs. A claim in these sources becomes a claim of
> the project only when a cut reproduces it and the index records
> the bytes. Until then it is cited here as what it is: the vision
> the measurements are chasing.

## 🗂️ Provenance

These six documents are the original Moviola Protocol content,
written before a line of the MCP server existed, moved here
verbatim (`git mv`, history preserved) when the repository gained
its code. Nothing in them was edited to agree with the
implementation after the fact — where the product corrected them
(the sub-5 MB long-form figures, now resolution-conditional facts
in the main README), the correction is printed in the README and
the original text stands untouched below it. That is what sources
are for.
